# Contributing to GBADuo

**Where to report:** bugs and feature requests go to this repository's
[issue tracker](https://github.com/khumam/gba-duo/issues). Companion packs, and
issues about a specific pack, belong in
[gba-duo-companions](https://github.com/khumam/gba-duo-companions).

**Pull requests:** welcome. Match the surrounding style, keep one change per PR,
and make sure the build and tests pass (`./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest` and
`app/src/test/native/run.sh`).

**AI assistance:** GBADuo is developed with AI help, and AI-assisted
contributions are fine. You're responsible for reviewing and testing what you
submit.

**License:** GBADuo is free software under the [GNU GPL v3](LICENSE). By
contributing, you agree your contribution is licensed under it too.
